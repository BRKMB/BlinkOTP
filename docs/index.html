BlinkOTP

Instant verification codes from Gmail — private, local, and automatic.

BlinkOTP is a Chrome extension that reads recent verification emails from Gmail (read-only) and helps you autofill one-time codes on websites you visit. All processing happens on your device. We do not run backend servers and we do not collect analytics.
